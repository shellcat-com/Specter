# Specter walkthrough

The 74.7-second final recording is copied byte-for-byte from the reviewed September 11, 2026 fast edit. H.264, 1600×900 at 30 fps, stereo AAC, fast-start MP4. The poster is a frame from that recording.

Real Specter sessions show synthetic content. Typing and generation are accelerated. Claude Code and DeepSeek are external CLI services, not Specter integrations. The food delivery result is a frontend prototype with simulated orders. The original generated food images and original synthesized music/keyboard Foley are part of the demo; no commercial sound samples were imported. Descriptive captions and a written walkthrough accompany the player. Direct sound audition was unavailable during the final edit; visual and audio-level checks passed.

Raw recordings and edit timelines stay in the local Specter-showcase artifact directory; only this reviewed final export and poster are published.
